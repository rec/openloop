# openLoop rererises repriseses in Rouen

## Next show: 17 October 2026, 14h - 19h at Le Diable Au Corps

## What?

America collapsed. We fled.

Amsterdam was not a great place to meet musicians but Rouen is just crawling with musical talent.

Let's do it again but different and better and with newer technology, where we record
every track of the audio and stream the mix live.

Bring electronic or electrical instruments. There will be some mics for quiet instruments or voice: we're aiming for a quiet stage, not a silent one.

Live loopers and delay heads particularly welcome.
