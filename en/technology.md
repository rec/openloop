# Technology

## Audio is excellent

* 24 input channels split between 2 cheap digital mixers mixed down to stereo
* A little Raspberry Pi without a screen records and streams automatically
* It records each channel or stereo pair, the mix, and the submix, a glorious 28 channels in total
* All is automatically uploaded in full quality sometime after the show

## Streaming is low bandwidth

* The current streamer is Twitch
* The audio is the mix
* There is no camera: the Raspberry Pi is primarily recording, and should not also process live video
* Before the show, a film is prepared using a Python script using randomization, so it's different every time
* The film slowly fades between short, curated abstract videos released into the public domain
* Participants get a QR code that lets them upload images that are faded into the video.
